How does this work?

This app uses patterns, keywords and a small phrase dictionary. It does not understand every letter. Requests are excerpts, not verified advice. Dates and amounts are copied as written, not calculated. Links are never opened or checked.

Nothing is sent to a server by this app. Text stays in this tab until you clear it or close it. Your browser or device may still retain information outside this app.
